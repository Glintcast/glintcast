# Reporting Issues

Found a bug, a Twitch layout break, or a readability issue? Open an issue at https://github.com/descentiles/glintcast/issues.

## What to Include

- **Edition & version:** Store or Direct, plus the version number (e.g. `0.3.0`).
- **Browser:** Chrome, Brave, Edge, Arc, or Opera.
- **Other Twitch extensions:** FFZ, BTTV, 7TV, or None.
- **What happened:** Short description of what broke vs. what you expected, plus a screenshot if visual.

## Feature & Accessibility Ideas

Keep ideas focused on the problem you want solved. Glintcast stays calm, local-only, and native to Twitch in dark and light modes.
